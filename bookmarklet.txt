HOW TO CREATE A ONE-CLICK SYNC BOOKMARKLET
===========================================

1. Right-click your browser's bookmarks bar
2. Click "Add page" or "Add bookmark"
3. Name it: "Sync Forza Photos"
4. In the URL field, paste this ENTIRE line below (it's all one line):

javascript:void((async function(){const C="dqwnsi211",P="forza_unsigned";console.log("Forza Gallery Sync starting...");const S=['img[src*="forzamotorsport.net"]','img[src*="forza.net"]','img[src*="ugcorigin"]','img[src*="xboxlive.com"]','.gallery img','[class*="gallery"] img','[class*="photo"] img'];const seen=new Set(),imgs=[];S.forEach(s=>document.querySelectorAll(s).forEach(i=>{let src=i.src;if(src&&!seen.has(src)&&(!i.naturalWidth||i.naturalWidth>100)){seen.add(src);imgs.push(src)}}));if(!imgs.length){alert("No Forza photos found on this page!");return}if(!confirm(`Found ${imgs.length} photos.
