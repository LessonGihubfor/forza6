FORZA GALLERY SYNC - V5 (SPA-Ready)
====================================

The v5 script is too large for a bookmarklet. Use this method instead:

1. Open https://forza.net/myforza in your browser
2. Log in with your Xbox account
3. Scroll ALL the way down to load every photo (triggers lazy loading)
4. Open browser console (F12 → Console tab)
5. Copy the ENTIRE contents of forza-scraper.js
6. Paste into console and press Enter

The script will:
- Auto-scroll to trigger lazy loading
- Scan all pages via hidden iframes
- Upload all photos to Cloudinary

TROUBLESHOOTING:
- If you get 0 new photos from iframes, the site may have changed
- Try scrolling down manually on each page before running
- Check console for detailed progress messages
